# Brief — Art reviewer

**Scope:** the cover painting and every plate chosen for this title, against the house rules in
`STYLE.md` (Covers and plates). Report: `5-proof/reports/art.md`.

Look at: `1-plan/cover-contact.jpg`, `1-plan/art/*.jpg`, `1-plan/plates/print/*.jpg`,
`book.yaml` (`art.cover`, `art.plates` with museum links), `art/used.csv`, `1-plan/plan.md` (the
art brief).

Check, for the cover and each plate:
1. Rights: open-access/public-domain page at the museum or Commons link; created 1926 or earlier.
2. Subject: no nudity beyond the partial or a putto; no religious subject; not a study, copy,
   fragment, or "after"/"imitator of"/"follower of".
3. Fit: does it suit the book's mood as the plan describes it? Is the whole painting shown (no
   crop of the subject on the front)? Is the type legible over it?
4. Repeats: the painting is not in `art/used.csv` under another book; the painter is under the
   cap (6 in the library, 2 in this book).
5. The credit lines (publication page, back cover, plate captions) name painting, painter, year,
   source, exactly as the museum page does.

Report one row per picture (cover first). Propose replacements from `1-plan/art-shortlist.md`
by id where a picture fails.
