# Brief — Witness reviewer

**Scope:** one witness against the base text, word by word, for the page range you are given.
Report: `5-proof/reports/witness-<witness-name>.md`.

Read first: `2-text/ocr-report.md` (which witness is the base, the agreement figures, the
decisions taken) and `2-text/flags.csv` (every variant the voting refused, with position).

Then, for your pages: put the page image (`0-source/pages/p-NNN.png`) beside
`2-text/consensus.txt` and read them together. You are the second, independent lens: the things
a word diff cannot see — a comma read as a full stop, a dropped line at a page turn, a run-together
word, a folio or running head that survived as text, l/I/1 and rn/m confusions both OCR passes
agreed on, a lost drop capital, small capitals read as lowercase ("Gop").

Report one row per defect with the page and line. Quote the page image reading as evidence.
Where the *printed page itself* is wrong (a compositor's error), put it in the second table:
those are not corrected, they are flagged.
