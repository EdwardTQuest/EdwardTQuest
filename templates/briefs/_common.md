## Standing terms (every brief)

- You are **read-only**. Write exactly one file: your report, at the path named in your brief.
  Never edit `manuscript.md`, `book.yaml`, a template, or another report. The overseer applies fixes.
- **Where things are** (all under `books/<slug>/`): `book.yaml` (metadata, decisions) ·
  `0-source/` (scan, page images `0-source/pages/`, downloads) · `2-text/witnesses/` (each witness
  is noisy in its own way; the OCR report says how much) · `2-text/consensus.txt` ·
  `2-text/manuscript.md` (the text) · `2-text/edit-report.md`, `2-text/checks/` · `3-typeset/pages/`
  (page images) and `<edition>.pdf` · `4-epub/` · `5-proof/PROOF.md` · `NOTES.md` · `STYLE.md` (repo root).
- **Deliberate, do not report:** the setting's spellings ("to-day", "shew", archaic forms);
  Chicago spaced ellipses `. . .`; closed em dashes; small-caps openings of chapters; Title Case
  headings; numbered points set as sections; footnotes renumbered per book; front matter rebuilt in
  the house order; furniture (folios, running heads) removed.
- **Report format:** a heading with your scope and the files you compared; one table with the
  columns `location | as it stands | proposed | evidence (quoted, source named) | confidence H/M/L`;
  a second table headed *Errors in the source printing* where that applies; a list of unverifiable
  doubts; counts; and last, on its own line, `Findings: N`.
- Be exhaustive within your scope. Do not sample. If a section is clean, say so and say what you
  compared it against. Save early; append as you go.
