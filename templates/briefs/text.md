# Brief — Text reviewer

**Scope:** `2-text/manuscript.md` after `ath edit`, read in full, with `2-text/edit-report.md`,
`2-text/checks/unknown-words.txt` and `checks/names.txt` in hand, and the page images or the
source text beside you. Report: `5-proof/reports/text.md`.

1. The dictionary list: classify every entry — archaic form (keep), proper name (keep), Latin or
   other language (keep), OCR or transcription error (row in your table with the correct reading
   and its evidence). Do not skip entries.
2. The structure battery's findings (`edit-report.md`): confirm or dismiss each one.
3. A read of the text for what no list catches: a sentence that does not parse (a lost line), a
   paragraph joined across a page turn that should be two, a heading that is really a line of
   verse, a quotation opened and never closed, a note call with no note.
4. The house style (`STYLE.md`) applied consistently: ellipses, dashes, quotation marks,
   small-caps openings, headings in Title Case. Report a *class* of inconsistency once with a count.

Two tables: manuscript errors; errors in the source printing (flag, never correct).
