# AGENTS.md — one overseer per title, one specialist per stage

The shape that worked on the Centennial editions: one agent holds the whole book in view and
is the only hand on the files; specialists are read-only, exhaustive within a stated scope, and
write one report each. Specialists get better at their one job because their brief is stable
and their scope is narrow. A smaller model can be a specialist; the overseer benefits from a
stronger one but does not require it.

## The overseer (one per title)

Owns `books/<slug>/` end to end. Runs the commands. Fills `plan.md`. Applies every fix (to the
source, a rule, `book.yaml`, the template) and re-runs. Reads every report. Commissions the
specialists below at the points marked, gives each the brief from `templates/briefs/`, and folds
their reports into `5-proof/PROOF.md` and `changes.csv`. Writes `NOTES.md` and signs it when the
definition of done in PROOF.md is met. Never delegates a decision about the text.

## Rules for every brief

1. **Read-only.** A specialist writes exactly one file, its report. It never edits a manuscript,
   a yaml, a template, or another report.
2. **Where everything is**, with paths, and which sources are themselves noisy.
3. **What is deliberate — do not report it.** The house conventions that look like errors
   (spaced ellipses, small-caps openings, "to-day", the setting's spellings).
4. **What to look for**, as concrete families with an example each.
5. **How to report:** one table, fixed columns — location | as it stands | proposed | evidence
   (quoted, from a named source) | confidence H/M/L — then unverifiable doubts in a list, then
   counts, then *"Findings: N"* on its own line (PROOF.md reads that line). Be exhaustive within
   the scope; do not sample; if a section is clean say so and say what it was compared against.
6. **Two tables** where it applies: what the manuscript got wrong, and what the *source printing*
   got wrong. They are different conversations.
7. Save the report early and append; if interrupted, the overseer resumes the same agent by
   message rather than relaunching.

## Convergence

Stop adding lenses when two in a row come back in single digits. Record every lens run, its scope
and its count in PROOF.md. A title is done when the lenses that apply to it have each been run
and their counts recorded, not when a pass is "clean".
